# Camera Simple

A simple camera app for the Mac.

## Why

A MacBook doesn't come with a simple camera app you can use as a mirror or to take a quick photo. Camera Simple is that app.

It is also meant to be quick to reach: press Command + Space, type "camera", press Return, and the camera is open.

## Features

- Live, mirrored camera preview
- Take a photo with the shutter button or the Space key
- Choose the folder photos are saved to
- Switch between cameras, including external ones
- Zoom in and out
- Aspect ratios: Full (the camera's own shape, the default), 16:9, 1:1, 9:16; the window keeps the picture's shape

## Install

Download the DMG from the [latest release](https://github.com/ddfzhh/camera-simple/releases/latest), open it, and drag Camera Simple to Applications. Works on Apple Silicon and Intel Macs with macOS 12 or later.

The app is not notarized by Apple, so macOS blocks it the first time. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security and click "Open Anyway". This is needed once.
